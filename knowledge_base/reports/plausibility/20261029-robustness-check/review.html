Source check: reservoir records 157/158

The article reports wet-season ebullition of 3.94 × 10−8 mmol CH4 m−2 day−1. Multiplication by 0.16 gives 6.304 × 10−9 kg CH4 ha−1 day−1, matching record 158. The dry-season value also matches record 157. These are bubble-pathway measurements and should not be pooled with diffusive or total flux without explicit boundary metadata. Source agreement does not independently establish measurement validity. Original article; open full-text XML, Results ebullition paragraph.
