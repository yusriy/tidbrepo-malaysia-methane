## Independence before pooling

A shared site, investigator or period is a reason to inspect dataset identity, not proof of duplicate observations. Before a meta-analysis, record site coordinates/name, dates, apparatus, pathways, plots or towers, primary dataset identifiers and explicit reuse statements. Keep distinct paper pages even when they reuse observations; annotate the relationship and avoid double counting the same measurements.

No duplicate normalized DOI/title paper entries were found in this cleanup. This identity check alone cannot establish dataset independence.
