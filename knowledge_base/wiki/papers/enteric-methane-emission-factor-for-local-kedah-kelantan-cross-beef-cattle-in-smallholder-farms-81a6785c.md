# Enteric Methane Emission Factor for Local Kedah-Kelantan Cross Beef Cattle in Smallholder Farms of Malaysia

> Full-text synthesis checked against the source on 2 November 2026. This status does not approve new factors or change existing database records.

## Study and methods

Malaysian surveys (2021–2022) supplied farmer-reported weights for IPCC Tier 2 energy calculations. Intake parameters used IPCC defaults. These are **calculated factors**, not direct methane measurements.

## Source findings

Section 3.1 reports a breeding-cow factor of **39.7**, a bull factor of **52.2**, and a pooled factor of **46.1 kg CH₄ head⁻¹ yr⁻¹**. Table 3 instead prints **52.5** for the bull. Preserve this disagreement; neither the pooled value nor formatting resolves it.

## Interpretation and limits

Use for breed-specific inventory context, with mature-animal and intake assumptions. It does not establish measured emission distributions or factors for every age class.
