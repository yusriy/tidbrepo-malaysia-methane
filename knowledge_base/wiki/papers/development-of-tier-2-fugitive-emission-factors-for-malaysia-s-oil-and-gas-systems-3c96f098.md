# Development of Tier 2 Fugitive Emission Factors for Malaysia's Oil and Gas Systems

> Full-text synthesis checked against the source on 2 November 2026. This status does not approve new factors or change existing database records.

## Study and methods

Jamaludin et al. analysed 2020–2024 operating data from 87 Malaysian oil/gas assets. Venting and flaring use gas composition; flaring assumes 98% efficiency. Other fugitives use component activity and API leak-rate factors. Normal operations exclude startup, shutdown, maintenance and breakdown events.

## Source findings

Table 3 reports methane factors:

| Source | Gg CH₄ / 10⁶ m³ |
|---|---:|
| Oil venting | 0.124 |
| Gas venting | 0.566 |
| Oil flaring | 0.00885 |
| Gas flaring | 0.00928 |
| Gas production, other fugitives | 8.97 × 10⁻⁵ |
| Gas processing, other fugitives | 9.56 × 10⁻⁶ |
| Gas transmission/storage, other fugitives | 1.09 × 10⁻⁴ |

Oil production/upgrading other fugitives instead use **1.11 × 10⁻⁷ Gg / 10³ m³**.

## Interpretation and limits

Check Table 1 activity definitions alongside Table 3: volume denominators are not interchangeable. These estimates combine measurements and engineering assumptions; they are not uniformly direct facility-flux measurements. Abnormal events and transmission/storage uncertainty need separate treatment. Keep activity boundaries explicit; converting volume-normalized factors to energy requires calorific information. Existing database values and units remain unchanged.
